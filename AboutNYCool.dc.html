Our Story

Fifty Years of Curation

On March 1, 1977, Judy Prince founded JB Prince from a brownstone in Park Slope, Brooklyn, on the theory that New York's chefs deserved direct access to the tools their trade actually demanded — not what a general houseware catalog happened to stock.

Two years later, Larry Prince left corporate America to join full-time — a six-month commitment that became a lifelong partnership. Judy brought product expertise and chef relationships; Larry brought the business discipline to grow it. Together they built the direct-to-source model that still defines how we buy today.

Fifty years on, under Tim Mussig's leadership since his start on the customer service desk in 2000, JB Prince carries the same standard forward — independently owned, chef-rooted, and still refusing to stock anything we wouldn't hand a chef ourselves — a standard now open to anyone who cares about cooking well, not just the professional kitchen.
