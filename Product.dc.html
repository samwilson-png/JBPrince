FAQ

What is a Pacojet used for?

The Pacojet is a powerful kitchen tool designed to quickly transform frozen ingredients into velvety purées, smooth ice creams, vibrant sauces, and hearty soups. Using a specialized blade system, it shaves thin layers from frozen food, maintaining its original texture and flavor without the need for thawing.

What is the Smart Device Detection Mode?

Smart Device Detection Mode enhances the user experience by automatically recognizing and adapting to a connected device — like a smartphone or tablet — optimizing the interface and providing enhanced control.

How do you clean a Pacojet?

Disassemble the parts, including the blade assembly and beaker. Rinse each piece with warm water to remove food residue, clean thoroughly with a mild detergent, rinse again, and air dry before reassembling and sanitizing with a food-safe sanitizer.
